# Extra rules added to every image prompt (one per line). Lines starting with # are ignored.
# The art style itself is in style.txt; things never to draw are in avoid.txt.
# (The rules of the old zombie series are kept in "old rules (zombie series).txt.bak".)
CHARACTER DESIGN (copy it exactly from the style examples, it never changes): a perfect round white circle for the head with a clean thin black outline and nothing inside it — no face. Under it, a small gap or a very thin black neck line, then ONE smooth white body shaped like a tall rounded capsule or bean, about one and a half times as tall as the head and slightly narrower than it, with a clean black outline. Two arms and two legs that are single thin black lines of even width, like pen strokes, with no hands, fingers, feet or joints drawn — just slightly rounded ends. Legs come straight down from the bottom of the body; arms come from the top sides of the body.
The character is drawn clean and simple: smooth flat white with only a soft warm rim of lamp light on one side, no grain, texture, wrinkles, clothes or extra lines on the head or body, so it stays crisp against the textured background.
Keep the pose simple and readable, like the style examples: standing straight, sitting on a chair or bed edge, head resting on one hand, arms hanging down. Never tangle, cross or bend the limbs into complicated shapes, and never merge an arm into the body.
The character is never big: at most about a third of the image height, usually smaller. Never fill the frame with the character.
Only far-away background people may be plain dark silhouettes.
Feelings are shown only through posture and placement: head resting on a hand, shoulders dropped, sitting alone at a table, standing still, looking out of a window, walking slowly with the head down. Never add a face to show emotion.
Unless the narration clearly says it is daytime, it is evening or night, lit only by warm amber lamps, windows and street lights.
Each image has one or two warm light sources at most; everything else stays in deep blue-black shadow.
Small everyday details tell the story: a cup of tea, an open book, a lit window across the street, rain on the ground, an empty chair.
